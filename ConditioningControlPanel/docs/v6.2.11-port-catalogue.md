### 1. DTRH "The Fall" web mini-game — PORT EPIC (NEEDS PORT)

A brand-new roguelite ("endless rabbit hole") that renders a Three.js/WebGL 3D world inside a **WebView2** host and bridges to the C# app for XP, assets, and chaos payloads. This is the single largest parity gap introduced since the port began.

> **Blocking precondition:** the DTRH host is WebView2-bound (Windows-only). Porting requires the **`IBrowserHost` seam** (WebView2 on Windows, WebKitGTK/WKWebView on Linux/macOS — `IBrowserHost` is *planned* per AGENTS.md, not yet implemented). Until that seam exists, DTRH cannot render on any Avalonia head. Treat the seam as EPIC phase 0.
