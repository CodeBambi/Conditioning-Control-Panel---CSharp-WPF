- AT LAND, the deltas are summed, never guessed:
    node client/tests/floor/sum-deltas.mjs --check  --packets SP-0NN-a,SP-0NN-b
    node client/tests/floor/sum-deltas.mjs --apply  --packets SP-0NN-a,SP-0NN-b
