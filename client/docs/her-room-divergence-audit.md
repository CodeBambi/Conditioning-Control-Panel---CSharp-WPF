# Her Room + Awareness divergence audit (SP-060)

**Date:** 2026-08-12 · **Task:** SP-060 (task-board row "Her Room + Awareness: reconcile the port's AI companion against upstream's shipped design (v6.7)") · **Shape:** decision input, not decision — zero product code, zero contract edits (SP-050 audit shape).

While the port built its AI companion in slices c1–c7 (SP-033/035/038/040/042/044/046 + SP-047 memory→prompt wiring), upstream shipped its own companion redesign: the Companion tab became **Her Room** with a new brain, opt-in awareness, a plain-words privacy dial, an app picker, incognito detection, a one-hour pause, and a Mute Voice Lines switch. This document compares **user-observable outcomes** element-by-element and assigns exactly one verdict per element.

**Verdict vocabulary (pinned by the packet — four values):**

- **ADOPT** — the audit found no reason to keep the port's version (or no port version exists) and no privacy/consent/logging/network boundary is broadened. Includes **subtractive** elements (things that remove or narrow data flow) — narrowing a boundary is not broadening one.
- **KEEP** — the port's landed behavior already delivers the user-observable outcome by different mechanics, or the divergence was **already decided** with a citation (not silently reopened), or no counterpart is needed by evidence.
- **MERGE** — the port has a counterpart, and a specific upstream mechanism/semantic has merit worth incorporating without replacing the port's architecture.
- **BLOCKED-ON-OWNER** — evaluated **first**, as a hard filter (constitution: never broaden webcam/capture/logging/consent/network boundaries; packet framing (a): consent defaults, retention, moderation policy, and user-visible product identity are owner decisions). Status quo preserved; the question lands in §4.

**Reading rules:** every upstream claim cites `File.cs:line` under `ConditioningControlPanel/` (paths abbreviated `CCP/`); every port claim cites `client/src/CcpClient.Desktop/` (abbreviated `client/`) plus the contract section (`ai-operation-contract.md` §N) or slice record. An element neither side could source is an explicit UNKNOWN row (§6). Each row's **boundary line** states what adopting the element would newly observe / retain / transmit — "none" is an answer.

## 7. Surprises worth keeping (durable-lesson candidates)

1. **The marketing model and the code model differ.** "Three privacy levels" is presentation over four booleans + two lists; the shipped default (empty title allow-list) is narrower than the dial's middle position implies. Future sync archaeology should expect marketing-shaped descriptions and enumerate the tree.
2. **A shipped asset can be dead.** `awareness_apps.json` has zero callers; its own comment confesses it mirrors embedded dictionaries. File-count deltas in sync ledgers do not imply behavior.
3. **Upstream drifted toward the port's posture on charging.** v6.7 burns cooldowns on delivery only; the port faithfully ported the old burn-on-attempt discipline. Not every port↔upstream difference is a port gap — this one is upstream fixing itself after the port snapshotted.
4. **The strongest adopts are subtractive.** Incognito drop, title scrub, deny lists, wire-view transparency — the elements that *remove* or *expose* data flow carry no privacy-filter pressure at all. Audits should hunt them deliberately.
