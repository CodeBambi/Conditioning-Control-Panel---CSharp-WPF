How XP Works

XP is the app's points system. Almost everything you do earns some: watching a session through, popping a bubble, finishing a quest, just opening the app on a new day. Enough XP and you level up.

Levelling up does three things. It hands you a sparkle point to spend on upgrades, it moves you up the leaderboard, and past level 30 it quietly increases how much sessions pay out.

The same XP does a fourth job on the way past, because it also fills the daily jar that decides whether today gets banked on the Descent. That is a separate count with its own seven stages, it reads nothing but banked days, and you do not have to do anything differently to feed it. There is a summary further down and the whole thing is written up in descent.log.

Nothing in the app is locked behind a level. Since v5.8.0 every feature is available from level 1, so XP is a score and a pacing mechanism, not a gate. Nothing is ever taken back either, which is worth saying plainly because it did not always work that way: your level, your XP, your streak and everything you have unlocked carry forward, and they will keep doing that from here on.

Three words worth knowing

Base XP - the raw number before anything is applied to it. Every figure on this page is a base number unless it says otherwise.
Global multiplier - a single number from the Skill Tree that scales every XP award you get.
Session multiplier - an extra multiplier on top, based on your level, that only applies to finishing a session.

You need to be signed in

XP is only awarded when you are logged into your CC Labs account, or running in offline mode with an offline username set. Use the app fully signed out and nothing is counted.

XP Sources

Sessions

Sessions are by far the biggest XP source. Every session has a base payout set by its difficulty, and that base then gets scaled by your level and topped up by a bonus for how long the session ran.

Session Base XP

The base is capped at 2,500, and every time you pause costs you 100 XP:

base = min(2500, sessionBaseXP) - (pauses × 100)

It cannot go below 0. Abandoning a session part-way awards nothing at all. Base XP by difficulty:

Difficulty Typical Base XP Examples
Easy 400 XP Morning Drift
Medium 800 XP Random Drop, Gamer Girl
Hard 1,200 XP Good Girls Don't Cum, Deep Dive
Extreme 2,000 XP Bambi Time
Remote Control 200 XP Someone else is driving
Custom (Timeline) Varies minutes × 10 + feature bonuses, rounded to the nearest 50
Training Program day 300 - 1,000 XP 300 + 700 × intensity, then 1.5x on a boss day

Duration Bonus

Sessions longer than 2 minutes award extra XP based on duration and level:

Duration Bonus = (minutes - 2) × (8 + level × 0.15)

Example: Level 50, 30-minute session
  = (30 - 2) × (8 + 50 × 0.15)
  = 28 × 15.5
  = 434 bonus XP

Final Session XP

Final = round(base × sessionLevelMultiplier) + durationBonus

Order matters here. The session level multiplier only touches the base, never the duration bonus. The global Skill Tree multiplier is applied last, to the whole thing.

Bubble count duration scaling

Clips shorter than 60 seconds pay proportionally less, so a 30-second clip pays half. The floor is 10% of base, so a very short clip still pays something.

One companion takes XP away

The Brainwashed Slavedoll, unlocked at level 125, drains 3 XP off your total on every tick while she is active. That is deliberate - the point is to out-earn her. Your XP is floored at 0, so she cannot put you in the red, but she can hold you still if you idle.

Open the app on a new day and your login streak goes up by one. That is the whole requirement - no session needed. With the Milestone Rewards skill bought, the streak pays you every day:

30+ days300 XP741 XP
 
Streak Bonus = base × (1 + (level - 1) × 0.03)

Example: 30+ day streak at Level 50
  = 300 × (1 + 49 × 0.03)
  = 300 × 2.47 = 741 XP

There is a second, separate streak: the quest streak, which counts days you finished a daily quest. With the Perfect Bimbo Week skill, hitting a quest streak milestone pays a lump sum:

Pink Rush

Pink Rush

Everything is worth 3x for 60 seconds. The whole sum above gets tripled, not just the base.

When it fires: once you own the Pink Rush skill, the app rolls a 50% chance every 10 minutes.
How long: 60 seconds, then it ends by itself. It can fire again later in the same sitting.

Maximum Multiplier

Maximum Multiplier

Without Pink Rush: 2.1x. That is all three Sparkle Boosts (+45%), Streak Power maxed out at a 30-day streak (+15%), and either Night Shift or Early Bird (+50%).
With Pink Rush: 6.3x. The 2.1x, tripled.

The session level multiplier stacks on top of that. At level 212 or above, during Pink Rush, in the small hours, a session base is worth 5.0 × 6.3 = 31.5x. That is the ceiling, and it needs a lot of things to line up at once.

Leveling

XP Per Level

Each level costs more than the last. The rate changes at four points, and past 150 it goes exponential. There is no level cap.

Level Range XP Per Level Formula
1 - 80 800 → 2,500 800 + (level - 1) × (1700 / 79)
81 - 100 2,500 → 4,000 2500 + (level - 80) × (1500 / 20)
101 - 125 4,000 → 6,000 4000 + (level - 100) × (2000 / 25)
126 - 150 6,000 → 10,000 6000 + (level - 125) × (4000 / 25)
151+ 10,000+ (exponential) 10000 × 1.03^(level - 150)

Total XP to get there

Level 25 is about 25,000 XP all in. Level 50 is about 64,500. Level 100 is about 194,000. Level 150 is about 516,000. Level 200 is about 1.64 million. Past 150 each level costs 3% more than the one before it, so the curve stops being a curve and starts being a wall.

Prestige

Prestige is a second number that sits alongside your level. It is not a reset button and there is nothing to click - it counts the sparkle points you have spent on skills, over your whole account life:

Prestige rank = 1 + (lifetime sparkle points spent / 100)

Spend your hundredth point and you hit Rank 2, and it climbs from there for as long as you keep buying skills. It is a spending record rather than a scoreboard, so it never goes down and nothing ever takes it off you.

Level Up Sound

Drop an lvlup.mp3 into Resources/ or assets/audio/ and the app will play it when you level up.

The Descent

Levels measure how much you have earned, and the Descent measures how long you have kept coming back. The two run side by side off the same XP, so nothing you read above is spent twice or split in half, and there is no separate grind to take up.

The short version is the one the app puts on the Trainer Card: everything you earn today lands in the tap, and you hold the tap to pour it into today's jar. The jar's capacity is set by the server and grows with your level so it never goes stale, and once the jar passes a fifth of that cap the day is banked. Banked days are the only thing that moves you down the Spiral, which is what makes a quiet evening that just clears the line count exactly as much as a heavy one.

the day is banked once  jar > 20% of today's cap

Seven stages sit along your banked-day count, opening at days 1, 7, 21, 50, 100, 180 and 300, and they pull the app's palette a little deeper each time. Time away isn't wasted either, since your fill rate rises 4% for every day you miss up to a ceiling of double, and that raised rate is what you're pouring at for your first three days back.

Nothing on this page resets any more

The app used to wipe level, XP and half the skill tree every month, but that's finished and the server suppresses the wipe permanently, so your level, your XP, your streak and everything you've unlocked carry forward, and they'll keep doing that from here on. The monthly leaderboard table still rotates, and that's the only thing that does. The whole system is written up in descent.log.

Fair Play

Idle Suppression

Leaving the app running while you go and do something else does not earn XP. After 3 minutes with no keyboard or mouse input, the app marks you idle and stops paying out the passive stuff. Anything that needs a click or a keystroke keeps working, because doing it proves you are there.

Suppressed When Idle Always Awards XP
Flash Images Bubble Pops (requires clicking)
Subliminal Messages Video Attention Checks (requires interaction)
Bouncing Text Lock Card (requires typing)
Bubble Count (requires answering)
Mantra (requires typing)

Rate Limits

A few activities are cheap and repeatable, so they have ceilings. None of these will bother you in normal use:

  • Bouncing text: 2-second cooldown, 150 XP per minute
  • Bubble count: 3 minutes between paying games
  • Mantras: 1.5 seconds minimum between them, 20 per minute
  • FYP clips: 12 paying clips per minute
  • Graded Intake: 100 XP total, however well you do
  • Chaos runs: score capped at 250 per minute, times the difficulty multiplier
  • Sessions: base capped at 2,500, minus 100 for every pause
The server checks your numbers

XP and stats are validated on the server before they sync. Impossible jumps, broken values and nonsense data get rejected. See leaderboard.htm for what that means for rankings.
