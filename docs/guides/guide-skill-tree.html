Overview

The Skill Tree is where you spend the points you earn from playing. It lives in the Enhancements tab. Every skill you buy stays bought, and most of them make XP come in faster.

It is not really a tree so much as three parallel chains. All three start from one root skill, and inside each chain you have to buy the skills in order - you cannot skip ahead.

  • Stats chain - unlocks numbers, records and rankings to look at
  • Sparkle chain - straight XP boosts, lucky rolls and Pink Rush
  • Streak chain - streak bonuses, streak insurance and quest rerolls

Sparkle Points

Sparkle points are the currency. They are separate from XP: XP raises your level, and levels hand you sparkle points to spend here.

  • 1 point per level up. No exceptions, no bonus points for big levels.
  • 1 point per 100 bubbles popped. Popping counts toward this all the time, in or out of a session.
  • Your point balance never resets, and neither does anything you have spent it on.
  • There is no refund and no respec. Once a skill is bought, that is that.
You need to be signed in to buy

Purchases go through the server, so you have to be logged into your CC Labs account. The server also holds the real point balance - if your local count and the server disagree, the server wins.

Nothing here is ever taken back

Older versions of this manual said that the XP-economy skills (the Sparkle chain, the Streak chain, the quest skills, Night Shift and Early Bird Bimbo) were stripped every month and had to be bought again. That treadmill is gone, so every node you buy is yours permanently, the display skills and the XP skills alike, and the only thing that turns over at the start of a month is the leaderboard table. See descent.log for what replaced it.

Tier 1 - Foundation

One skill, and everything else hangs off it. Buy this first.

Shows your total conditioning time across all sessions

Tier 2 - Core Branches

The three chains start here. All three need Pink Hours, and you can buy them in any order.

🔥💖 Good Girl Streak 5 pts

Rerolls come from two different chains

Quest Refresh and Better Quests sit at the end of the stats chain. Reroll Addict sits at the end of the streak chain. If you want all three rerolls a day, you have to finish both.

The PRO Analytics Branch

Five more stat skills sit past Better Quests, at the far end of the stats chain. They are wildly expensive and they only show you information - none of them affect XP. They are also all permanent, so a season rollover never takes them away.

Ditzy Data PRO 150 pts

The full lifetime dashboard, with every stat the app has ever counted

Requires: Better Quests

Season Rewind 250 pts

Compare each season against the last

Requires: Ditzy Data PRO

Bestie Records 350 pts

A timeline of your personal bests, all-time and per season

Requires: Season Rewind

Brain Drain Report 500 pts

A breakdown of how much you have used each feature, this season and ever

Requires: Bestie Records

Certified Data Bimbo 1,000 pts

The capstone. Full prestige history per season, plus a certified badge

Requires: Brain Drain Report

2,250 points for the set

This branch alone is nearly nine tenths of the cost of the whole tree. It is a long-haul goal, not something to save up for early. Buy everything that actually pays out XP first.

Secret Skills

Three skills sit outside the tree, on their own row underneath it. They have no prerequisites, so you can buy them at any point - but until you meet the unlock condition, all you see is a card marked ??? with a hint.

🌙😴 Night Shift 10 pts

+50% XP between 11pm and 5am

Unlock: start a session 10 times in the 11pm-5am window

🌅☀️ Early Bird Bimbo 10 pts

+50% XP between 5am and 8am

Unlock: start a session 10 times in the 5am-8am window

💎♾️ Eternal Doll 8 pts

Shows your lifetime stats across every season

Unlock: reach level 50 in any season

The in-app hints are slightly off

Night Shift's hint says "after midnight" and Early Bird's says "before 7am". What the app actually counts is app launches between 11pm and 5am for Night Shift, and between 5am and 8am for Early Bird. Only the first launch of a session counts, so opening and closing the app repeatedly will not speed it up.

Eternal Doll survives seasons, the other two do not

Night Shift and Early Bird Bimbo are XP skills, so they get stripped at the season rollover along with the rest. Eternal Doll is a display skill and is yours for good.

Pink Rush

Pink Rush is the last skill in the sparkle chain and the biggest single multiplier in the game. It fires on its own, at random, and triples everything for a minute.

  • Costs 25 points, at the very end of the sparkle chain
  • Roughly a 50% chance to fire every 10 minutes while the app is running
  • Lasts 60 seconds, then ends on its own
  • Everything you earn in that window is worth 3x
  • It can fire more than once in a long session
It multiplies your other bonuses too

Your XP boosts add together first, then Pink Rush triples the whole total. With all three Sparkle skills, maxed Streak Power and a time-of-day secret skill running, that adds up fast. See progression.log for the exact math.
