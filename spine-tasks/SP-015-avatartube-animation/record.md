## Step 1 — pre-approach consult verdict (solo; requested `anthropic/claude-fable-5` — council unavailable T-7)

**Actual answering model: Fable family, consistent with the requested route (T-7 discipline).** Verdict (applied, binds Steps 2-5):

1. **Pause gate MUST observe the generation token** (`WaitAsync(token)`) — a paused engine whose gate ignores cancellation wedges teardown's drain and breaks the zero-unobserved rule.
2. **Fixed-quantum engine loop (~16ms) with MONOTONIC-clock (Stopwatch) deadline accumulation** — not per-frame `Task.Delay` chains. Frame N appears at cumulative-delay deadline ± quantum; deadline-based scheduling absorbs Task.Delay's systematic 1-15ms overshoot (Windows) instead of accumulating drift — load-bearing for cadence falsification. Pause freezes deadline advancement; resume re-bases (`deadlineBase += pauseDuration`) and waits out the remainder of the current frame's delay — resume semantics pinned EXACTLY so "cadence unchanged" is assertable.
3. **Float:** step from the same monotonic clock every quantum — stepping float only on frame ticks (80-300ms) would be visibly choppy and couples float cadence to clip delays (WPF's 16ms tick exists for a reason). One operation, one clock, one loop.
4. **Engine-stepped opacity CONFIRMED** (vs Avalonia Transitions): pause freezes the crossfade automatically; the never-blank invariant (`opacityA + opacityB >= 1 - epsilon` at every step) is pure math, unit-testable; ~60 posts/s through IUiDispatch is nothing. Transitions would be a second timing authority the engine can't pause — rejected.
5. **Capture-sampling correction:** PS `CopyFromScreen` costs 50-200ms jitter per capture; 80-300ms holds cannot be sampled reliably. ALL synthetic clip delays must be >= ~400ms (non-uniform, e.g. 430-900ms) so the slowest capture cadence samples every hold >= 2x for the no-blank/dup-run gates. Multiplied-speed falsification becomes a **schedule-fit test**: observed (t, index) pairs must fit the 1x cumulative-delay schedule within tolerance AND must NOT fit 2x or 0.5x schedules — non-uniform delays make the schedules distinguishable (why the packet bans uniform delays).
6. **Duplicate-pipeline falsification:** add **monotonic-modular-advance** assertion (each observed frame is a forward step within its clip; an interleaved second pipeline produces backward jumps) + registry outstanding==1 corroboration during the leak long-run.
7. **Temporal math lives in CcpVerify, not the script:** new pure sequence evaluator (samples JSON -> named PASS/FAIL verdicts: advance, no-blank, monotonic-modular, no-dup-run-beyond-hold, schedule-fit-1x, schedule-not-2x, schedule-not-half-x, resume-successor, pause-freeze, pack-switch-clean), unit-tested like CheckEvaluator (SP-008 pattern). The PS script stays dumb: capture -> decode -> append -> call evaluator once. Cadence tolerance windows are machine-checked, never hand-held PowerShell.
8. **Crossfade strip-blend trap (false-blank):** mid-crossfade the two layers' strips BLEND and strip decode fails — a decode failure there is NOT a blank. During engine-declared crossfade windows (known from the frame trace) the no-blank gate = union-of-visible-content (incoming OR outgoing OR blend non-blank); strip assertions apply outside crossfade windows. The engine logs each applied frame (pack/clip/index + monotonic ticks) to ILogSink as a **frame trace** — corroboration and crossfade-window knowledge, never standalone liveness evidence (REJECT lesson stands).
9. Under-thinking flagged: teardown ordering of the pause gate (point 1) and the false-positive risk of naive blank checks (point 8) — both designed in above.
