## Ghost mode, from source

**It is not a transparency setting.** It is the OnTopReplica technique: the real WebView2 window is
**parked off the virtual desktop** and a separate **GDI/WinForms** window paints a **live DWM
thumbnail** of it over the feed's home monitor, translucent and click-through
(`FypGhostOverlay.cs:9-42`). The obvious approach is recorded as a shipped failure at `:14-17` —
`SetLayeredWindowAttributes` on a WebView2 host "turns its content solid BLACK". Colour key is
RGB(1,1,1) not black; alpha rides `DWM_TNP_OPACITY`, never window alpha; the two clickable controls
are separate tiny topmost windows because the mirror is entirely `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT`.

**Privacy-relevant: yes, and flagged in its own section rather than folded into a size** — though the
surface's sharpest privacy facts turned out not to be ghost mode at all (below).
